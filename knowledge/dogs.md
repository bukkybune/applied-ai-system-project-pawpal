# Dog care basics

## Daily needs of adult dogs
Most adult dogs do well on two meals a day at roughly the same times, fresh water at all
times, and at least two chances to go outside and exercise. A predictable routine helps
dogs settle, and many behaviour problems such as chewing or barking get worse when a dog
is bored or under-exercised.

## Exercise for dogs
How much exercise a dog needs depends mostly on breed, age and health. Many adult dogs
need somewhere between 30 minutes and 2 hours of activity a day, split across walks and
play. High-energy working and herding breeds need the top of that range. Signs a dog is
not getting enough exercise include restlessness, destructive chewing and weight gain.
Avoid hard exercise straight after a large meal; resting for about an hour after eating
is a common precaution, especially for large, deep-chested dogs, because of the risk of
bloat.

## High-energy dog breeds
Breeds bred for working or herding usually need 60 minutes or more of exercise a day,
plus training or puzzle games for their minds. Examples include the Siberian Husky,
Border Collie, Australian Shepherd, Belgian Malinois, Labrador Retriever, Golden
Retriever, Jack Russell Terrier, Vizsla, Weimaraner, Dalmatian and German Shorthaired
Pointer.

## Senior dogs
Dogs are often considered senior from around 7 to 10 years old, earlier for giant breeds.
Older dogs usually still need daily walks, but shorter and gentler ones. Watch for
stiffness, weight change, drinking more water than usual and changes in appetite, and
mention them to a vet. Many vets suggest check-ups every six months for senior pets.

## Dental care for dogs
Daily tooth brushing with a toothpaste made for dogs is the most effective home dental
care. Never use human toothpaste, because some contain xylitol, which is toxic to dogs.
Bad breath, red gums or dropping food can be signs of dental disease and are worth a vet
visit.

## Grooming dogs
Grooming needs vary by coat. Double-coated breeds such as Huskies and Corgis shed heavily
and benefit from brushing several times a week, more during seasonal shedding. Nails
usually need trimming every few weeks; if you can hear them clicking on the floor they
are probably too long.
