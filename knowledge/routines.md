# Suggested starting routines

These are starting points, not prescriptions. Every animal is different; a vet can tailor
a routine to your pet's health, age and breed. PawPal reads the lines below to suggest
tasks, so keep the format: `title | minutes | priority | repeats | covers | window`.

- `repeats` is `daily`, `weekly:<days>` (for example `weekly:sat` or `weekly:mon,thu`),
  or `interval:<days>`.
- `covers` is the care area the task looks after.
- `window` is optional, `HH:MM-HH:MM`.
